# Style references (Redpillars, 2026-10-10)

> Collected for Pillow Battles and carried over to Ragdoll Battlegrounds (2026-11-23) as the default style target.
> Confirm with Redpillars that the vibe still applies before the first Ragdoll Battlegrounds map.

Redpillars said: "Have a look at all the images in this folder and train your building skill with those. That's the
exact vibe I'm going for." Earlier, about the first picture: "don't take it 1:1, it's more so the vibe I'm trying

**Open the images with the Read tool before every build.** This file is the written breakdown. The images
themselves are the target.

| Files | What they show |
|---|---|
| `rooftop-01` … `rooftop-07` (.jpg) | **Map A:** a daytime construction-site rooftop in a city, with a soft painted, anime-style look |
| `arena-01` … `arena-07` (.webp), `style-target-01.png` | **Map B:** a futuristic neon arena. `style-target-01` and `arena-05` show the same "03" building |

The source files were named `11000ft-*`, so both maps probably come from the same builder or studio. They look
completely different on the surface, but they're built the same way. **That shared craft is the vibe.** The
themes are only examples of it.

## 1. The shared DNA: rules that hold in both maps

1. **Big, simple, chunky shapes.**
   - Everything reads from far away: crates are fat cubes, pipes are fat cylinders, and a building is a few big
     boxes and slabs.
   - Almost nothing is smaller than a hand, and edges are crisp.
   - The craft is in proportion and arrangement, not in small parts.
2. **A cool base with a few warm accents.**
   - Map A is all blue-grey, white and navy, with orange crates and planks, coral cranes, raspberry brick walls,
     pink pipe stacks and one cyan band.
   - Map B is charcoal, navy, white and light grey, with one bold colour per building (orange or blue) plus lime,
     yellow, cyan and orange glow.
   - **Warm against cool, above all orange against blue, is the signature.**
3. **Horizontal slab architecture.**
   - Thick slabs stick out past the walls, often with **rounded corners seen from above** (Map A's helipad
     building and all of Map B's buildings).
   - **Dark caps over light walls:** navy roof slabs and dark trims sit on top of lighter walls, giving strong
     dark and light bands.
4. **Clean, light, stylized textures:** light brick on walls (white or peach), square floor tiles with a soft
   grid, and metal panels with seams and small screw dots. Textures are low-contrast and tidy, never dirty or
   gritty.
5. **Big openings.**
   - Map A's office has huge window walls with a dark grid of window bars, reflecting the sky.
   - Map B has deep dark doorways and near-black glass in thick frames.
6. **Iconic props in cover clusters.**
   - Props are chunky, simple and instantly recognisable.
   - They're grouped in **clusters of 2-5 that work as cover**, with open lanes between the clusters.
   - Each prop has a **darker frame or rim that reads like a cartoon outline**:
     - Map A's crates have dark edges, and the pipe ends have a light rim.
     - Map B's cover crates are dark frames around light grey inset panels.
7. **Graphic decals instead of words.**
   - The helipad "H" in a circle, yellow warning triangles on the floor, and yellow-black hazard stripes.
   - Big building numbers (02, 03, 04), line-art emblems and lane lines.
   - **No words anywhere.**
8. **A vivid sky and a strong sun:** a saturated blue sky with big stylized clouds, strong sunlight and long,
   crisp, blue-tinted shadows. The shadows are part of the design, because they break up the open floors.
9. **Depth through haze.** Distant things are simplified and fade into blue:
   - Map A surrounds the rooftop with a skyline of plain blue towers: boxes with vertical light strips.
   - Map B's background towers are glowing silhouettes.
10. **Low-poly nature, where it appears:**
    - Faceted pine trees in layered tiers.
    - Gem-shaped faceted shrubs in small square planters.
    - Tufts of grass on dirt mounds.
    - **Faceted, dark slate-grey rock cliffs** (`arena-04`).
11. **Fences frame the play area:** chain-link with white razor wire in Map A, dark slatted fences in Map B.

## 2. Map A: daytime construction rooftop (`rooftop-01` … `rooftop-07`)

**Palette (approximate):**

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Floor tiles | light blue-grey `#AEB8CC`, grid lines `#9AA6BC` |
| Walls | white or light-grey brick `#E4E6EE`, peach brick `#F2C9B6` |
| Caps and trims | navy `#2C3A5C`, deep navy `#1F2942` |
| Accents | raspberry `#C8305A` (brick walls, kiosk bands), orange `#F08A3C` (crates, planks), coral `#F06A5A` (cranes), pink `#E8506E` (pipes) with a pale-blue rim, cyan `#29A9F0` (one band) |
| Hazard | yellow `#F2C230` and black |

**Architecture:**
- **The office block:** 3-4 floors.
  - Thin, light floor slabs stick out at every floor line.
  - Light-brick columns run between huge window bays.
  - The windows are a dark grid of bars over big panes that reflect the sky.
  - The ground floor is glass, and you can see a lit interior with crates inside.
- **The helipad building:**
  - A rounded, stadium-shaped footprint, with peach brick walls over a navy base band.
  - A dark navy roof slab that overhangs, with a white "H" in a circle and yellow warning triangles.
  - An outside staircase with dark railings leads up to the roof, which is part of the play space.
- **Small blocks:**
  - Dark navy brick boxes, each with a cyan or raspberry band near the top and a flat dark cap.
  - Kiosks with raspberry bands.
  - A navy-brick block raised on cyan legs.
- **Unfinished columns:** concrete posts with yellow-black hazard stripes at the base and steel bars sticking
  out of the top.

**Props:**
- Coral lattice tower cranes, with loads hanging on cables (pallets of planks, bundles of pipes).
- Orange crates with dark edges, stacked in pyramids.
- Pallets of planks, pipe stacks in pyramids, and white plastic road barriers.
- White scaffold trestles, stacks of plasterboard, and broken raspberry brick walls with ragged tops.
- Chain-link fencing with dark posts, white bands and white razor wire.
- A few scattered planks and white plaster spill patches on the floor.

**Ground and backdrop:**
- Blue-grey tiles, a darker lane with dashed white lines, and a sunken cobbled courtyard reached by stairs.
- All around, a skyline of plain blue towers fading into haze, with the sea at the horizon.

## 3. Map B: futuristic neon arena (`arena-01` … `arena-07`, `style-target-01`)

**Palette (approximate):**

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Base | charcoal and navy `#1E2A44` / `#2B2F38`, white and light grey `#E6E9EF` / `#C9CED8`, steel-grey columns |
| Building colours | orange `#F08A12`, blue `#1E7BE0` |
| Glow | white, lime `#B8F000`, yellow `#FFD23A`, cyan `#2FD6FF`, orange `#FF8A1A` |
| Solid accent | a plain lime cover block `#B8F000` |

**Architecture:**
- **Two-storey buildings:**
  - Thick dark slabs and roofs with rounded corners and **white glowing edge strips**.
  - Small square lights set into the dark undersides of the slabs.
- **A thick white round column** with panel lines holds up the corner of the roof that sticks out. It stands on
  a dark base.
- **Wall details:** cladding with panel seams, **big numbers** (02, 03, 04) in a squared-off sci-fi font, white
  line-art emblems, white louvred panels, and a big dark garage door with the number on it.
- **Roof shapes:** curved dark shells on top, like half-pipes and fins.
- **The server tower:**
  - Stacked rounded floors with **lime glowing slots** and green-lit interiors.
  - Red-and-white striped antennas with lights on the tips.
  - A vertical green light beam.
- **Light towers:** tapering stacks of white glowing slats, like a pagoda, and rounded towers with pale yellow
  glowing rings.

**Props:**
- Cover crates: a dark frame around light grey inset panels, with screw dots.
- Low dark barriers with notches, and the plain lime block.
- Gem shrubs in square planters, low-poly pines, and a dark slatted fence.
- Grass tufts on small dirt mounds, and a faceted rock cliff at the edge.

**Ground:**
- Blue-grey metal tiles with screw dots.
- **Curved white walkways** set flush into the floor, with smooth rounded bends.
- **Glowing lines** in cyan, orange and yellow, running along the paths and up to the doors.

## 4. How to build these pieces in Roblox

- **Rounded slab with a glowing edge:** see "How to build the reference's pieces" in `SKILL.md`.
- **Cartoon-outline props:** build the prop's edges as a separate darker frame and inset lighter panels into it,
  or the reverse. Don't leave a prop as a single flat box.
- **Window wall:** a big pane (light-blue Glass or SmoothPlastic, Reflectance about 0.3), with a grid of dark bars
  in front of it. Light-brick columns between bays. Thin light floor slabs sticking out at each floor line.
- **Pipe stack:** cylinders in a pyramid, each with a lighter ring at both ends for the rim. Pink or raspberry
  body with a pale rim.
- **Tower crane:** a lattice mast and boom made from chunky thin beams in coral. The Pillow Battles City crane
  (`city/04_Construction.luau`) and the Port map cranes (`portmap/03_Cranes.luau`), both in the Pillow Battles repo
  (see SKILL.md, "Worked examples"), are starting points; the City one needs recolouring and chunkier beams. Hang a
  pallet load on four cables.
- **Chain-link fence:** dark posts with white bands and white feet. For the mesh, use a see-through part with a
  diamond-pattern Texture (this needs an image asset), or a coarse lattice of thin white Detail bars. Razor wire
  is a looping row of thin white squares. It costs a lot of parts, so use it only on the fence runs players see
  up close.
- **Skyline backdrop:** plain boxes tinted blue, with vertical lighter strips, placed far out and left to fade in
  the haze. Decoration only, out of reach.
- **Faceted rock:** rotated wedges and boxes in dark slate grey with sharp facets, never smooth spheres.
- **Gem shrub:** two pyramids base to base (corner wedges), bright green, in a small square concrete planter.
- **Low-poly pine:** layered flat tiers that get smaller toward the top, each tier turned a little, on a short
  trunk.
- **Floor decals:** thin flat Detail parts. The helipad "H" is a ring of short segments plus three bars. Warning
  triangles are built from wedges, and hazard stripes are alternating yellow and black strips.
- **Curved walkways:** white rounded slab pieces set 0.06 studs proud of the floor (the house z-fight stand-off; this
  line originally said 0.02, which z-fights: see SKILL.md, "Z-fighting"). Build the curves the same way as the
  rounded slab corners.
